# Alpha hunt: range-bound crosses, daily z-score fade (`cross_zfade`)

Rules, variants and the survival bar are in the docstring of `run_alpha_cross_zfade.py` (fixed before running). 5 crosses (AUDNZD, EURGBP, AUDCAD, NZDCAD, EURCHF excluding entries 2011-09-06..2015-01-15), signal = bid close of the 16:44 NY minute, market entry and non-stop exits at the 16:45 NY open, SL 1.5 x ATR20 (Z9 2.5), exit on the z-score crossing 0 or a 10 / 20 trading-day time stop. All costs included ($7/lot, 0.2 pip on the entry and stop exits, FRED swap with 1%/yr markup). Periods by signal day: design 2010-17, check 2018-23, holdout 2024-26; last 3 years = 2023-10-08..2026-10-07.

**Judged tests: 9 (pooled 5-pair result per variant). Charter bar on the pooled result (design month-clustered t >= 2, check and holdout avg R > 0): 0 (none). Survivors (bar AND >= 4 of 5 pairs with avg R > 0): 0 (none). Expected by luck: 0.05.**

Per-pair tests (information only): 45; passing the charter bar: 0 (none); expected by luck 0.27.

Best pooled design month t: Z3 (N=50 k=2 SL 1.5ATR) -0.49.

## Verdict and reading

**No edge. None of the 9 pre-declared variants comes close to the bar.** Every pooled variant has a negative design-period
avg R (month t from -2.04 to -0.49) and a negative net avg R over 2010-2026 (-0.086 to -0.013). Before costs the results
are about zero as well (gross avg R -0.034 to +0.040; costs take 0.026-0.055 R per trade). So costs are not what kills it:
the reversion is not there.

- **Win rates are 36-53%, not the 60-70% practitioners quote for mean-exit fades.** The 1.5 x ATR20 stop is hit on 43-58% of
  trades (21% with the 2.5 ATR stop in Z9). A daily close 2 SD from its 20- or 50-day mean on these crosses tends to keep
  going for a few days before any reversion. Trades that reach the mean exit make +1.1 to +2.1 R (+0.67 R in Z9), but the stops outnumber them.
- **Pairs:** only EURGBP is positive in 8 of 9 variants (avg R +0.04 to +0.23, design t at most 1.28, so no per-pair
  pass). AUDNZD, the textbook range pair, is the worst: negative over 2010-2026 and in the design period in all 9 variants. The count of pairs
  with avg R > 0 is 1-2 for 8 variants and 4 only for Z4, whose design avg R is -0.147.
- **The ER < 0.3 filter (Z5-Z8) does not help.** It cuts trades by 12-52%. Pooled avg R is about unchanged for N=20 (Z5 -0.054 vs Z1 -0.055; Z6 -0.053 vs Z2 -0.072) and worse for N=50 (Z7 -0.052 vs Z3 -0.025; Z8 -0.086 vs Z4 -0.013).
- **Regimes:** six variants sold EURGBP at 16:45 NY on 2016-06-24 (the Brexit result day) and were stopped out on
  06-27 for about -1.0 R each. The stop capped the loss; the five worst trades of every variant lose between -1.10 and
  -1.62 R; gaps through the stop and costs push a few past -1 R. AUDNZD 2022-23 was about flat for 6 of 9 variants. Z2, Z6 and Z8 lost 0.41-0.56 R per trade there, on 3-8 trades.
- **EURCHF floor:** with the pre-declared exclusion, no variant had EURCHF exposure on 2015-01-15. Illustration only: Z1 with
  the floor period included had no position at the 09:30 UTC de-peg. It would have bought at the 16:45 NY close that day
  (z = -4.25) with a 19.7-pip stop, sized from floor-period ATR, and been stopped one minute later for -1.05 R. Its 47
  floor-period trades lost 15.5 R in total.
- **Rollover spread:** the 16:45 NY spread is about 1.4-1.5x the 12:00 NY spread (AUDNZD 3.1 vs 2.2 pips), but that is
  only 1.5-3.3% of the stop distance. It is not material.
- No variant survived, so no prop sim was run.
