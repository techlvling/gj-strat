All costs included ($7/lot, 0.2 pip slippage on stop fills, bid/ask fills, swap with 1% markup). **Judged tests: 10. Survivors of the charter bar (design t >= 2, avg R > 0 in check and holdout; FV pooled also >= 3 of 5 pairs positive): 0 (none). Expected by luck: 0.06.**
